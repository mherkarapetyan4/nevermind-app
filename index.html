How it works

Just the next task Add everything you need to do. Nevermind shows you only one thing, in order of what matters.
No guilt, no resets No broken streaks, no punishment. Set a task aside and come back to it later.
Hard to avoid Snooze a task three times and it moves to the top. No more quietly dodging the same thing.
Always in view Your current task on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, so you don't forget to open the app.
